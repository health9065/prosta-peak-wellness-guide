# Prostate Health Guide

Maintaining a healthy lifestyle can support overall wellness as men get older. Regular physical activity, balanced nutrition, adequate sleep, hydration, and routine medical checkups are important parts of a healthy lifestyle.

## Healthy Lifestyle Habits

Some general habits that can support men's health include:

- Eating a balanced diet with vegetables, fruits, whole grains, and lean protein
- Staying physically active on a regular basis
- Maintaining a healthy weight
- Drinking enough water
- Getting adequate sleep
- Avoiding tobacco
- Limiting excessive alcohol consumption
- Discussing persistent urinary or prostate-related symptoms with a healthcare professional

## Prostate Health

The prostate is a gland in the male reproductive system. Prostate-related changes can become more common with age. Symptoms such as frequent urination, difficulty starting urination, weak urine flow, or getting up frequently at night should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional.
